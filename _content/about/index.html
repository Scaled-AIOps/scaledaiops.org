About ScaledAIOps

An open, community-maintained framework for how organisations work with AI.

What is ScaledAIOps?

ScaledAIOps is a governance-first framework for adopting AI tools such as coding assistants, agents and copilots across the SDLC, CI/CD and DevOps, safely, measurably and at scale. What SAFe did for agile at enterprise scale, ScaledAIOps does for AI adoption.

It covers the whole lifecycle, from business concept to a running production system and on to its retirement. It does so by restructuring the practices organisations already run, not by adding a parallel "AI process".

The name reads as a scaled operating model for AI. ScaledAIOps is not an AIOps product or practice, the category of tools that apply analytics to IT-operations data.

About using AI, not building it

Most organisations are not training models. They are adopting AI tools inside delivery work they already do. Until September 2026 ScaledAIOps covered building and operating AI systems; that edition is kept, unchanged, in the legacy archive.

Why this exists

AI tools spread through engineering teams faster than the rules for using them. Organisations struggle to say which tools touch which code and data, who owns AI-assisted work, and whether any of it improves delivery. Regulated industries also need to show their controls work. ScaledAIOps gives teams, platform groups and risk functions a shared vocabulary and a proportionate set of controls they can adapt to their context.

Our approach

  • Practitioner-led: built by practitioners from regulated industries, rigorous enough for a bank and practical enough for a startup.
  • No vendor lock-in: tool-agnostic and vendor-neutral. The framework names capabilities and controls, not products.
  • Proportionate: four risk tiers scale every control, so low-risk work stays fast and high-risk work stays safe.
  • Anchored: mapped to the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001, without tying either to a tool.
  • Open and evolving: CC BY-SA licensed and changed in the open as practice matures.

This site practises the framework

Every page is tracked in Git. AI-assisted edits are reviewed by a person before they merge, and their commits carry a vendor-neutral AI-assisted: yes trailer, so the history shows what was assisted and who approved it.

How to contribute

ScaledAIOps is hosted on GitHub under the Scaled-AIOps organisation. You can contribute by:

  • Submitting improvements or new practices via pull requests
  • Opening issues to discuss practices, propose changes or report gaps
  • Sharing how you applied the framework in your organisation
