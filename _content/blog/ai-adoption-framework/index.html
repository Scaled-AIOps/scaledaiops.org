ScaledAIOps is now a framework for working with AI

From building AI systems to adopting AI across the delivery practices organisations already run.

Published 23 September 2026

What changed

The first edition of ScaledAIOps described how to build and operate AI systems: training platforms, model lifecycles, data operations. That is real work, but it is not the work most organisations are doing. Most are not training models. They are adopting AI tools, such as coding assistants, agents and copilots, inside the software delivery they already run.

That adoption raises questions the first edition did not answer. Which tools may touch which code and data? Who owns a change an AI drafted? What must a person approve, and what may run on its own? How do you show a regulator, or your own second line, that the controls work? And is any of it making delivery faster, better or cheaper?

From today ScaledAIOps is a governance-first framework for exactly those questions.

Restructure, don't replace

The framework does not add an "AI process" next to the one you have. It keeps your lifecycle, from business concept through specification, generation, verification, release and operation to retirement, and changes what happens inside each stage. Code review becomes risk-tiered. Commits carry a vendor-neutral provenance trailer. Pipelines gain licence, eval and policy gates. Agents become non-human identities with owners and expiry dates. Prompts and context become versioned assets that are retired like anything else.

The new model

LayerWhat it gives you
LifecycleNine stages from Conceive to Retire, an element register and an AI-aware pipeline
Six disciplinesAI-Augmented Delivery, Human–AI Workflow Design, Tooling & Context Engineering, Governance & Guardrails, Skills & Roles, Value & Measurement
Four control pillarsAssess, Prevent, Prove, Detect, scaled by four risk tiers
Maturity modelSix dimensions by five levels, with a self-assessment that runs in your browser
Principles and rolesEight of each, from "Human accountability, always" to the Value Analyst

Controls are proportionate. A startup doing low-tier work and a bank doing critical-tier work use the same model at different settings. Where regulation matters, the framework anchors to the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 without tying either to a tool.

Where the earlier edition went

Nothing was deleted. The first edition's disciplines, principles, roles and posts moved unchanged to the legacy archive, and their old addresses point there.

How this change was made

The repositioning followed its own rules. It was specified first in a design document, drafted with AI assistance, reviewed by a person before merge, and every commit carries an AI-assisted: yes trailer.
