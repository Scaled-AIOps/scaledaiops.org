Core Disciplines

Six interconnected disciplines for building, deploying, and operating AI systems at scale.

How the 6 Disciplines Map to the AI Lifecycle

Unlike monolithic software frameworks, ScaledAIOps organizes organizational capability into six clear disciplines. Each discipline addresses a distinct operational requirement — from upstream data governance and model training platforms to continuous inference observability and enterprise AI strategy.

ML Engineering & Platform

Infrastructure, tooling, distributed training, and serving engines that enable teams to build, train, and host models with high reliability and low latency.

Model Lifecycle Management

End-to-end governance of models from experimental notebooks through model registry approvals, artifact versioning, rollout gating, and retirement.

Data Operations

Ensuring data quality, immutable lineage, schema contracts, feature store reliability, and privacy governance to fuel trustworthy AI systems.

Reliability & Observability

Real-time telemetry, data and concept drift detection, AI-specific SLOs, incident response playbooks, and automated retraining triggers.

Security, Ethics & Compliance

Responsible AI practices, prompt injection threat modeling, fairness testing, explainability cards, and regulatory adherence (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF).

Strategy & Organization

Aligning AI investments with business value, structuring cross-functional team topologies, measuring ROI, and scaling AI literacy across the enterprise.
