Disciplines

Six things an organisation must be good at to adopt AI safely, measurably and at scale.

How the disciplines fit together

Each discipline is one dimension of the maturity model. Each states which control pillars apply at each risk tier, and what changes in the practices you already run. Delivery is the work itself; workflow, tooling and skills shape how it is done; governance and measurement keep it safe and show whether it pays off.

AI-Augmented Delivery

AI in every stage from business concept to retirement, an AI-aware pipeline, and an element register that gives every artefact an owner.

Human–AI Workflow Design

Who does what: delegation by risk tier, approval gates, agent autonomy levels, handoffs and escalation paths.

Tooling & Context Engineering

Selecting and integrating tools, including through MCP, agents as non-human identities, and prompts and context as versioned, reviewed assets.

Governance & Guardrails

The four control pillars applied to AI use: policy, inventory, third-party risk, policy-as-code and the three lines.

Skills & Roles

The competency shift to specifying and verifying, redefined roles, team topology, enablement and AI literacy.

Value & Measurement

Cycle time, rework, defect escape, review burden and total cost, measured for teams and flow and never for individuals.
